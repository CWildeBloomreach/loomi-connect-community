# Event vocabulary and likely queries

Use these as a starting point. Always add the team's own wording: internal campaign codes ("BF26"), the names on banners and emails, and the categories the event links to.

## Words to match rule, campaign and banner names

| Event | Name keywords |
|---|---|
| Black Friday / Cyber Monday | black friday, bf, cyber, cyber monday, cm, bfcm, doorbuster, deal, deals, weekend |
| Seasonal sale | sale, clearance, outlet, markdown, % off, final reduction, mid-season, end of season, summer, winter |
| Holiday gifting | gift, gifts, christmas, xmas, holiday, stocking, advent, hanukkah, secret santa |
| Valentine's, Mother's, Father's Day | valentine, mother, mum, mom, father, dad |
| Product launch | the product or collection name, launch, new, drop, pre-order, preorder |
| Back to school | back to school, bts, school, uniform, college |

Matching is case-insensitive and on whole words where possible: "bf" should not match "bfly".

## Queries shoppers type

Test the first five for every event, then the event-specific ones. Run them one at a time.

**Generic deal queries:** sale, deals, offers, discount, clearance, outlet.

**Black Friday / Cyber Monday:** black friday, black friday deals, cyber monday, bfcm, doorbusters.

**Gifting:** gifts, gifts for him, gifts for her, gifts for mum, gifts for dad, gifts for kids, stocking fillers, gifts under 50, gift card, gift set.

**Launch:** the product name, common misspellings of it, the collection name, "new in", "new arrivals", "pre order".

**Category plus deal:** "<top category> sale", for example "dresses sale", "shoes sale". Pick the top 3 categories by revenue.

## What good looks like

- Generic event terms ("black friday", "cyber monday", "sale") usually work best as a **redirect** to the event landing page, scheduled for the window.
- Gift queries should land on gift-relevant products, not just anything with "gift" in the title. A relaxed query (for example "gifts for him" relaxed to "gifts") is a sign that a gift guide or ranking rule is missing.
- Event names that aren't real words get **spell-corrected** unless they are in the catalog or added as a keyword. In testing, "black friday" was auto-corrected to "black frisky" on an apparel account with no Black Friday content and returned black clothing.
